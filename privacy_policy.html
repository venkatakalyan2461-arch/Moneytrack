Privacy Policy

SpendSmart — Track Expense & Budget

🔒 100% Private

Your Data Stays on Your Device

SpendSmart does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All your expense data, budgets, and settings are stored locally on your device only. We have no access to your financial information.

What We Store

SpendSmart stores the following data locally on your device using browser storage: transaction amounts, merchant names, categories, dates, and notes. Budget limits and app preferences. Your display name for personalized greetings. All of this data remains on your device at all times.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personal identifiers. We do not collect bank account details or UPI credentials. We do not track your location. We do not share any data with third parties. We do not display advertisements.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, SpendSmart sends local reminders from your device. These notifications are generated on your device and do not involve any external servers or data transmission.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by going to Settings → Data → Clear All Data. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data permanently.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at the email address listed on our Play Store page.
