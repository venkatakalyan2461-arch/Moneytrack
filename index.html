Privacy Policy
🔒
100% Private
Your data never leaves your device
What We Store
SpendSmart stores your expense data, budget settings, and preferences locally on your device only. All data remains on your phone at all times.
What We Don't Collect
We do not collect your name, email, phone number, bank details, or UPI credentials. We do not track your location. We do not share data with third parties. We do not display advertisements.
Notifications
Notifications are generated locally on your device. No data is sent to any server for notifications.
Data Deletion
You can delete all data anytime via Settings → Data → Clear All Data. Uninstalling the app also removes all data permanently.
Contact
For questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the email listed on our Play Store page.
